# Adviser and finance sessions

Use the [first-session worksheet](STAFF_SESSION_WORKSHEET.md) for two 20-minute
sessions, participant prompts, timing and an observation table.

Open **Workspace tools → Staff usability**. Each participant records the task,
completion, elapsed seconds, error count, whether help was needed and comments.
Participants see their own observations; managers and administrators can review
their workspace's observations. These entries remain separate from automated
test evidence. No actual staff sessions have been conducted for this change.

Prepare a test workspace with an adviser, a receiving adviser and finance staff,
plus a student, an application, missing documents and an unpaid invoice. Use
test data. Pick at least one record beyond the first list page and two similarly
named contacts. Participants should use their normal permissions.

| Participant | Task | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adviser | Find today's most urgent application and explain the next action. | Can they choose without help? Do the counts and deadlines make sense? |
| Adviser | Search for a student, review documents and a portal response, then update the next action. | Search success, context switching, ambiguous labels, lost selections. |
| Both advisers | Propose, review and accept a handoff. | Can both explain who is responsible before and after acceptance? |
| Finance | Search for an application, create an invoice and record a test receipt. | Record selection, remaining balance, payment approval confusion. |
| Either | Recover from a stale edit or failed integration, then repeat a key task on a phone. | Understandable recovery, keyboard focus, horizontal fit and touch controls. |

Start timing when the task is presented. Avoid coaching until a participant asks
for help; record that help. Capture the exact point of hesitation and an example
of the wording or control they expected. Do not record customer data or sensitive
participant information in comments.

Turn observed obstacles into reproducible issues: task, role, steps, actual and
expected behavior, impact on completion and frequency. Fix completion blockers
first, then repeated delays. Repeat affected tasks with staff and record a new
observation. Session completion and findings remain pending until actual staff
participate; the automated browser scenario uses explicitly labeled fixture
observations.
